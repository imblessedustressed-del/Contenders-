🥊 CONTENDERS BOXING LEAGUE

For anyone who grew up writing fight plans

If you played the Web Boxing League (WeBL), you already know how this works: build a fighter, write his plan, check the report after fight night. Contenders was started by a longtime WeBL player and keeps that core, then goes its own way.

What feels familiar

  • Fighters built from ability points, with height, build and weight classes.
  • Fight plans with aggression/power/defense lines and if-then conditions.
  • Weekly bouts, ratings and status, training points from wins, rankings and belts.

What's new

  • Simbot: watch any fight in a 3D ring, or challenge another manager live while others watch.
  • Three regions: Contenders, Rookies, and 25-round Bare Knuckles.
  • Purses, fans and pay-per-view that grow with your fighter's star power.
  • Corner Coach notes after every fight, and an Ask-your-AI helper for plans.
  • Our own fight engine, tuned from real results so every style has a real chance.

Contenders is an independent fan-made game and isn't connected to WeBL.
