# Contenders — maintainer notes

Contenders is a text-based boxing strategy game modeled on the old Web Boxing League (WeBL, webl.vivi.com). The owner is the GitHub user imblessedustressed-del. Claude acts as league commissioner and maintainer.

## Where things live
- **Live site:** https://contendersboxingleague.com (custom domain since Nov 10, 2026, registered at Porkbun by the owner; DNS: 4 GitHub A + 4 AAAA records and www CNAME to imblessedustressed-del.github.io; repo `CNAME` file). The old https://imblessedustressed-del.github.io/Contenders-/ redirects there. GitHub Pages, served from the `main` branch root.
- **Google Search Console:** verified Nov 10, 2026 (URL-prefix property https://contendersboxingleague.com/, owner's Google account) by the `google-site-verification` meta tag in `src/shell.html`. Never remove that tag. robots.txt and sitemap.xml are in the repo root.
- **Source:** `src/shell.html` (all pages, styles and UI code; contains the placeholder `%%SIM%%`), `src/plan.js` (WeBL fight plan language parser), `src/sim4.js` (round-based fight engine).
- **Build:** `sh build.sh` writes `index.html` = shell with plan.js + sim4.js inlined. Always edit `src/`, rebuild, then commit both `src/` and `index.html`.
- **Backend:** Supabase project `ojsphhzgzbxmnfcbitqb` (organization "Contenders", free plan). Email/password auth with email confirmation turned off. Site URL is set to the live site.

## Database (public schema, RLS on every table)
- `profiles(id → auth.users, gym unique, description)`. Created automatically at sign-up by the `handle_new_user` trigger from the `gym` user metadata.
- `gym_state(user_id, data jsonb)`. Each manager's saved fighters, records and plans. Private to the owner.
- `topics(forum, title, author → profiles, last_at, post_count)` and `posts(topic_id, author, body)`. Readable by anyone; members insert their own. A post trigger bumps `last_at` and `post_count`. Authors are set to null when a gym retires.
- `messages(sender, recipient, body, read)`. Only the sender and recipient can see a message. The `mark_messages_read()` RPC marks your inbox read.
- `gym_public(user_id, data jsonb)`. Each gym's public card (fighter names, records, rating/status, titles, recent fight results; never stats or plan text). Readable by anyone, written by the owner when his gym saves. Powers Gym Rankings and public gym pages.
- `bouts(fight_date, div, red_user/red_fid, blue_user/blue_fid, belt, purse, rounds, status, summary, result)`. Every Contenders bout is 12 rounds (owner rule, Nov 2026); Bare Knuckles bouts are 25. All test bouts (ids 1-5, Pen Sale vs Delgado/Morrow) and the test fighter Pen Sale were wiped on Nov 9, 2026 for launch; every record is 0-0. (Now done nightly by the league-runner edge function; see below.) Commissioner runs a bout by simulating it with the real engine, writing `result` (report data, fighter stats stripped), `summary` and status 'done', and updating the bot side directly; a manager's own client applies the result to his fighters once (`state.applied`). Scheduled league bouts. Readable by anyone; no write policies, so only the commissioner (SQL/service role) schedules or records them. Your Fighters, fighter pages and Fight Cards read the Next Bout from here.
- `profiles.location` is the gym's location shown in Gym Rankings.
- `retire_gym()` RPC deletes the caller's account.
- **Bot gyms:** twelve computer-run gyms (profiles.is_bot = true, ids 00000000-0000-0000-0000-0000000000b0..bb, no password so nobody can log in as them). Their gym_state is readable by everyone so players can spar with them in Practice Fights. Generators: `bots/gen.py` (b0-b3, 2-3 fighters per division spread across them) and `bots/gen2.py` (b4-b7, added Nov 9, 2026: Bayou Iron Works, Steel Valley Boxing, Desert Wind Fight Club, Harbor Lights Boxing; each has 5 fighters in every division, one of each type: Slugger, KO Puncher, Dancer, Counterpuncher, Clincher; data in `bots/bots2.json`); b8-bb added the same day the same way (`SET=2 python3 bots/gen2.py 7719 <taken-names.json>` → `bots/bots3.json`: Rust Belt Pugilists, Pacific Coast Boxing, Lone Star Fight Factory, Atlantic City Ring Club). `bots/pack_sql.py` turns a bots file into one SQL load script. In engine tests Counterpunchers win about 60% against the other types and KO Punchers about 30%; worth rebalancing once real fight data comes in. Weight formula: (160 + 9.2*(height_in - 68)) * build + 1.5*(STR-12) - 1.0*(AGL-12).
- **Engine source of truth:** WeBL's published rules page https://webl.vivi.com/eko/gory-details.html (owner-approved Nov 9, 2026). Energy points enter the damage formula raw (there is no separate table). Remaining ambiguity: landing constant k = 12 + (average of the two ratings)/3 is our reading of "1/3 of the two fighters' rating". Training and rating follow WeBL (glossary/FAQ): rating +1 per win (+2 on a winning streak), -1 per loss with exceptions (beaten by higher rating; split/majority decision unless title fight vs equal/lower rating); status = highest rating ever; 1 AP per status point gained, plus a bonus AP at status 6 and every even status after. Training (owner, Nov 12, 2026; no random points in Contenders): the AP a win earns go in order to `train` (primary), `train2` (secondary) and, on a fast-track win (winning streak), `train3` (third, picked before the fight, only shown while streak > 0; deleted when the streak ends). League runner must pass pre-fight ratings in `summary.ratings` = [red, blue].
- **Purses (owner-approved Nov 9, 2026):** pot = $2,000 x 1.35^(higher rating), x4 for title fights; even ratings split 50/50, otherwise 60/40 to the higher-rated fighter; a defending champion takes 65%. Debut 0-vs-0 pays $1,000 each. Star power multiplies the pot: (1 + 0.1 x career title-fight wins, max 20) x (holds 2 belts in a division 1.5, all 3 2.5); the bigger star in the bout sets it (`starPower()`/`boutStar()`; runner passes it as `window.STAR`). `boutPurses()` in shell.html; the runner stores `summary.purse` = [red, blue]; clients add it to `F.earn`.
- **Venues (owner-approved Nov 9, 2026):** `bouts.venue` {name, city, cap, tier} is picked when a bout is scheduled with `pickVenue(venueTier(...))`: star power 2x+ = stadium, title fight or a top-10 contender (rating 3+) = big arena, else club. The runner stores attendance in `summary.att` via `attendanceFor()`.
- **TV / PPV and fans (owner-approved Nov 9, 2026):** at scheduling set `venue.tv` = `broadcastTier(belt, star, contender)` ('ppv' | 'tv' | 'stream'; a 'tv' bout on a PPV night shows as the undercard). The runner stores `summary.fans` = fightFans(f, belt, tier) and `summary.tv` = broadcastResult(tier, fansBefore, ratingsBefore, homes, beltsHeld, purse) (buys/viewers, regions, pay). Clients add tv.pay to earnings, fans to `F.fans`, PPV buys to `F.ppv`. Fighters' PPV share = 25% of buys x $79.99, split like the purse; TV pays $0.10 per viewer.
- **Tyson Fury rule + simulator update 2 (owner, Nov 12, 2026):** no fighter taller than 6'9" (hgt <= 21) and the heavyweight limit is 280 lb (DIVS Heavy = 280; a heavier man sweats down like any division). The old small-man edge is OFF and WeBL's heavyweight numbers are replaced. All balance knobs are in `BAL` at the top of sim4.js: weight bonus +1 STR per 10% (was 5%), giant fatigue 0.5 per 10 lb of min weight over 200 (was 1), counter-punching AGL swing adv/6 (was /3) with no STR bonus and AGG x0.8, KP stun weight 3.75 (was 3), ring +1.75 AGL with 0.4 extra fatigue (was +1 / 1), clinch +1.75 AGL and 12% AGG smother (was +1 / 15%), all-out takes x2.75 damage (was x4). Engine tests (bots2 round robin, 9 divisions): Slugger 36%, KO Puncher 39%, Dancer 44%, Counterpuncher 38%, Clincher 45% wins (the rest draws); a pure all-out flasher wins about 35%; a 6'8" 275 lb heavy vs the usual 225 lb bots about 45-48%. Harness scripts were /tmp/bal.js, /tmp/hv2.js, /tmp/ao.js (round robins with BAL overrides).
- **Injury points (owner rule, Nov 9, 2026):** as WeBL (damage taken minus 5 x CHN is free; every 500 points costs a random ability point), except a fighter who is KO'd gets a flat 10 injury points instead of WeBL's 25.
- League bouts are run with `prepFighter()` + `simulate()` from the built index.html; the result's `ip` (injury points per corner) goes into `bouts.summary.ip`.

## Security (owner-approved Nov 9, 2026)
- `gym_state` is server-controlled for fighters: the `gym_state_protect` trigger lets players change plans, training, division, nickname and hometown, but restores name, record, rating/status/streak, abilities, earnings, fans, PPV, injury points and history from the old row, keeps `applied`/`champs`/`rsVer` server-owned, and only accepts brand-new fighters with exactly 70 ability points. Commissioner writes (no auth.uid) bypass it. It also never loses a retired fighter: old retired entries are always kept unless their fid returns to the roster, and any roster fighter with fights or earnings who disappears is auto-retired (Nov 9, 2026 fix after Pen Sale was dropped by a stale tab). Gym totals (record, earnings) include retired fighters. **So the commissioner now applies league results to human fighters too** (same as bots): after a bout, update both fighters' gym_state entries (applyResult in the built page), and the client no longer applies results itself.
- `gym_public_protect` rebuilds a manager's public card from his gym_state, so cards can't show fake numbers.
- Players can't change `profiles.is_bot` (column privileges: they may update gym, description, location only).
- Per-round tactics (energy points) are private: `bout_private(bout_id, user_id, mgr)`, readable only by that manager; `bouts.result` never contains `mgr`.
- Sanctions: `sanctions(user_id, kind gag|ban, strike, reason, ends, lifted)`. `select public.give_strike(uid, 'gag'|'ban', reason)` gives the next strike: 2 days, then 14 days, then permanent (lift with `update sanctions set lifted=true`). RLS blocks gagged users from topics/posts/messages and banned users from gym_state/gym_public. Owner's standing order: the commissioner may gag or ban on his own judgment (e.g. a known plan/gym thief called "dub"); the scheduled check stays report-only and only recommends.

## Retired champions and ducking (owner-approved Nov 9, 2026)
- A champion who retires keeps his belt until the next fight-scheduling run. **First step of every scheduling run:** `select * from public.vacate_retired_champs();` (commissioner only). For each champion not on his gym's active roster it records a row in `vacancies(div, belt, bout_id, ...)` (the site treats that title-winning bout as no longer holding the belt), removes the belt from his gym's `champs`, and resets his win streak (`ducked: true`). Then schedule a fight for each vacant title it returned (top contenders in that division).
- Contracts (owner, Nov 9, 2026): once a bout is scheduled it will be fought. A fighter with a scheduled bout (`bouts.status <> 'done'`) can't leave the roster: `gym_state_protect` puts him back and sets `retireAfter: true`. The manager can cancel that flag. **After running each bout, the commissioner moves any fighter with `retireAfter: true` to his gym's `retired` list (add `fid`, `div`; remove from roster).** (The earlier 'ducking costs the streak' rule was replaced by this.)

## Rules for maintenance
- **Transparency rule (owner, Nov 9, 2026):** every owner-approved change to the simulator, rules or any major gameplay change is posted to Contenders News (forum `announce`, topic and post with author NULL, shown as "The Commissioner") the moment it goes live. Say it was approved by the owner, and that the owner has no access to anyone's plans, gyms or fighters.
- Bot gyms only use public information (records, fight reports) to plan against a manager's fighter, never his plan text.
- The owner approves anything that changes gameplay, rules, the simulator, the schedule, titles, or a manager's fighters or records.
- The scheduled commissioner check is REPORT-ONLY: it reads and reports, and never changes code, the database or settings. Bug fixes are made only after the owner approves them.
- Never copy WeBL's text or graphics. Match its layout and mechanics in original wording.
- **League is LIVE (owner, Nov 9, 2026).** Every human manager's fighter gets one fight a week against a bot fighter in his division, on that division's fight night (`FIGHT_NIGHT`: Sun Straw/Junior Fly/Fly, Mon Super Fly/Bantam/Super Bantam, Tue Feather/Super Feather, Wed Light/Welter/Super Welter, Thu Middle/Super Middle, Fri Light Heavy/Cruiser, Sat Heavy). Booking happens at midnight Central (the 11:56 PM pass) right after that night's fights: tonight's fighters get next week's night; a night less than 24 hours away skips to the week after. No title fights until the owner says so. The league-runner edge function does this nightly (the old Claude task with `tools/league.py` is disabled): first `select public.vacate_retired_champs()`, then run every scheduled bout whose fight_date has arrived, then book next bouts.

## Server-side league runner (live Nov 12, 2026)
- Supabase edge function `league-runner` (source in `supabase/functions/league-runner/`: index.ts, fight.ts, core.ts). pg_cron job `contenders-league-runner` calls it at :11/:26/:41/:56 every hour with header `x-runner-key` = `runner_key.k` (private table); it only acts from 10:40 PM Central (fight night) unless `?force=1` (`&book=1` = booking only). Each pass logs a row in `league_runs` (ran, booked, vacated, note). Lock: `runner_begin()`.
- Pass: `vacate_retired_champs()`, then every scheduled bout dated today or earlier (title bouts are skipped and left for the commissioner), each recorded atomically by `commit_bout()` (service role only; same writes as tools/league.py), then (only at the midnight pass, 11:50 PM Central or later, owner Nov 12: "the scheduler schedules at midnight") booking: humans first vs the closest-rated free bot in the same division AND region, then bots vs bots (same division/region, different gyms). Bot-vs-bot bouts (`bouts.bot_only`) keep only the summary (result null). Bots book like everyone else (owner, Nov 12: "every bot and fighter for themselves").
- The function downloads the engine at run time from https://contendersboxingleague.com/engine-core.js, which `build.sh` generates with `tools/mkcore.js` (needs `cd tools && npm i` once). So any engine/rule change goes live for the runner as soon as the site is pushed. Redeploy the function only when index.ts/fight.ts/core.ts change.
- The old Claude scheduled task "Contenders league runner" (trig_018LL4x615reJ7yQKSdLR51z) is DISABLED; nightly fights no longer use Claude. tools/league.py is still the manual fallback.
- Site loads all human/title bouts, all scheduled bouts and the latest 400 bot results. Fight Schedule page lists upcoming bouts by night and the latest results.

## Fair matchmaking (owner, Nov 12, 2026)
Never book two fighters whose total AP (str+kp+spd+agl+chn+cnd+hgt+cut) differ by more than 1. Among fair pairs: same status first, then closest rating (18/18 vs 18/18, then 17/18, 16/18...), then status 1 apart, etc. If no fair opponent is free he isn't booked (logged in league_runs.note). Applies to humans vs bots and bots vs bots (lib.ts booking).

## Self-managing bot gyms (owner, Nov 12, 2026: "we want the bots to evolve, try to win... five active fighters in all regions... posting press releases at each other and with me")
- Runner code now lives in `supabase/functions/league-runner/lib.ts` (+ botgen.ts, press.ts, fight.ts). `build.sh` bundles it with esbuild into `/runner-lib.js` on the site; the deployed edge function `index.ts` is a tiny loader that downloads engine-core.js and runner-lib.js every run. So a site push updates the runner; redeploy only if index.ts changes. Test locally with a mock client (see git history: /tmp/mock.ts pattern).
- Learning: each bot fighter has `bt` (type) and `gene` {a, d} (AGG/DEF offsets applied to his type's plan template, safety lines fixed). After a loss by stoppage: d+1, a-1; by decision: a+1, d-1; draw: a+1; win: unchanged. Plan text is regenerated from the gene (`planFor`).
- Scouting: at fight time a bot's plan gets +1/+2 DEF if the opponent's public record shows a 30%/50%+ KO rate (2+ fights). His saved plan isn't changed by scouting.
- Rosters (midnight pass, before booking, gyms b4-bb only; b0-b3 are left as they are): retire a free bot if aploss >= 3, or 8+ fights and losses - wins >= 4, or 40+ fights (moved to `retired` with `why`); then sign new bots until each division has 5 active per region (regular: one of each of the 5 types; BK: Grinder/Clincher/Flasher spread). New bots inherit the gene of the best same-type bot in that division/region (small mutation). Nov 12: the first pass signed 512 BK bots (BK now 5 per division per gym = 640).
- Press releases, WeBL style (owner, Nov 12, 2026): forum 'press', topic title = the speaking fighter's name, first post = his one or two lines, author = his gym. The Press Releases page (`pressHTML`, route forum:press) is a single feed: Fighter (Gym) (date) + text, with a region filter (Contenders press / Bare Knuckles press, all weights) and a post box (pick one of your fighters, 280 chars). Managers can edit (posts: update(body) grant + 'authors edit posts' policy) or delete (existing author delete policies) their own press releases. Bots post trash talk only: after every result with a manager's fighter, up to 4 bot KOs/upsets a night, and a call-out when booked against a manager's fighter. No signing or retirement posts.

## Bare Knuckles region (live Nov 12, 2026, owner-approved)
- Fighter field `region: 'bk'` (absent = regular Contenders region), chosen at creation; trigger `gym_state_region_lock` makes it permanent. `bouts.region` ('bk' or null), `bouts.rounds` 25 for BK.
- Engine (`simulate`, BK when both prepped fighters have `bk`): 25 rounds; recovery 5% of lost endurance (10% normally) plus the usual rest bonus; no ringside doctor stoppage; both eyes swollen shut = forced clinch; going the distance: more knockdowns scored wins (a point deducted from the opponent counts as a knockdown), then more stuns, else Draw (`out.bkScore`). Rating/status cap 18 for BK fighters (28 regular).
- Separate rankings (`frank:Div@bk`), champions (`champs:bk`; currentChamps keys BK belts as 'BK '+div), help page `help:bk`. Practice/Simbot: two BK fighters always go 25 rounds under BK rules.
- BK bots: started as 128 (one per division in each of b4-bb, keys k0-k15), topped up to 5 per division per gym by the runner, from `bots/gen_bk.py 1225 <taken.json>` -> `bots/bots_bk.json` (loaded through pg_net from the site). Types rotate Grinder (ropes), Clincher, Flasher. Engine tests: Grinder beats Flasher, Clincher beats Grinder, Flasher about even with Clincher; Grinder mirrors draw often. Revisit with real data.

## Time zone (owner, Nov 12, 2026: "make sure everything's central time")
