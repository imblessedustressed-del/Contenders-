🥊 CONTENDERS BOXING LEAGUE

A text-based boxing strategy game for people who think about fights

Contenders is played in your browser with no download. It's a strategy game first: every bout is simulated round by round from your fighter's numbers and the plan you wrote, then written up like a ringside report, punch by punch.

Fight plans in plain language

A plan is a few lines. The first sets his normal tactics, and the lines below change them when something happens:

6/6/8 (inside)
if opponent <= tired then 7/7/6 (inside)
if hisstuns > 0 then 7/8/5
if endurance_percent < 40 then 4/4/12 (clinch)

Aggression, power and defense, plus a style: inside, outside, ring movement, counter-punching, clinching, ropes, feints or all-out. Good plans manage the gas tank, protect a lead and finish a hurt man. Bad ones gas out in round eight.

Learn it in minutes

Simbot lets you build two fighters and watch them fight right away, no sign-up needed. A Corner Coach sends tips after your league fights, and the help pages cover every condition and style.
