🥊 CONTENDERS BOXING LEAGUE

A free boxing manager game where your plan wins the fight

In Contenders you don't throw the punches. You run the gym. You build each fighter from the ground up, decide how he trains, and write the fight plan he follows round by round. Then he steps into the ring against fighters from other gyms and the result is decided by the plan you wrote.

How it works

  • Build fighters. Spend 70 points across strength, knockout power, speed, agility, chin, conditioning, height and cut resistance. Every build has a weight class and a style it suits.
  • Write the fight plan. Tell him how hard to press, how much power to put on his shots and how much to defend, and when to change: when he's ahead, behind, tired or has the other man hurt.
  • Fight every week. Each fighter gets a 12-round bout on his division's fight night, with a full round-by-round report.
  • Climb the rankings. Wins raise his rating, earn training points, purses and fans, and move him toward the title picture.

Watch it, don't just read it

Every fight can be replayed in Simbot, a 3D ring with a referee, ten counts, cuts, scorecards and a crowd that roars on every knockdown.

Three ways to fight

Contenders is the main league. Rookies is a gentler league for new managers learning the game. Bare Knuckles is 25 rounds with no gloves, scored on knockdowns.
